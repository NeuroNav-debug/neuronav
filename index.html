🧰 Regulation Toolkit

Pick your current state and get tools matched to it.

🔴 Your nervous system is in overdrive. The goal is to reduce input — not push through.
🌬️Box Breathing4 sec in → hold → out → hold. Repeat 4×
💧Cold WaterSplash face or hold ice cube for 30s
🤚5-4-3-2-1Name 5 things you see, 4 hear, 3 touch…
🪨Heavy InputWeighted blanket, push against wall, hug yourself tightly
🔇Reduce InputDim lights, noise-cancelling on, close tabs, quiet room
🌀Safe StimRock, hum, tap a rhythm, fidget — stims calm the CNS
🔵 Shutdown means your system needs gentle reactivation — not forcing. Start micro-small.
🐢Micro-MoveStand up. Walk to another room. That's enough.
☕Warm DrinkMake tea or coffee — activates sensory and routine systems
🎵Familiar MusicPlay a comfort playlist — activates memory & dopamine
1️⃣1 Task OnlyChoose the single smallest possible action. Do just that.
🧘Body ScanNotice tension from toes to head. Release each part gently.
☀️Light & AirOpen blinds or step outside — natural light resets circadian
⚡ Hyperfocus is powerful — but you need to protect your body and transition out gracefully.
🧍Body CheckHave you eaten? Drank water? Moved in the last 2 hours?
⏰Set Exit TimerSet a 25-min alarm — hard stop no matter what
🅿️Parking LotOpen a note, dump all unfinished thoughts — free your brain
🔄Transition RitualSame closing routine: save, note next step, shut screen
💧Hydrate NowHyperfocus suppresses thirst — drink a full glass right now
🙆Stretch BreakStand and do 5 shoulder rolls and neck stretches
🩷 Anxiety in ADHD often means your nervous system needs safety signals. Ground yourself first.
😮‍💨Physiological SighDouble inhale through nose, long exhale — fastest known anxiety reducer
🏠Safety ScanLook around: "I am physically safe right now." Say it out loud.
🏷️Name It"I feel anxious" — labeling an emotion reduces amygdala activation
📝Worry DumpWrite every worry down — externalizing clears working memory
🚿Cold SplashCold water on face triggers dive reflex — slows heart rate fast
💜RSD CheckIs this real or RSD? Check your Vault to reframe the trigger

✨Novelty InjectChange location, background music, or lighting immediately
🏃Move Your BodyJump, dance, sprint in place for 60 seconds
🎯Add ChallengeMake the task harder, faster, or gamified to activate ADHD drive

💡 How to use this board
NOW — the single thing you're doing right now. Keep it to 1–2 items max.
NEXT — what you'll do when Now is done. Don't think about it yet.
LATER — everything else. Safely parked. Not your problem right now.
